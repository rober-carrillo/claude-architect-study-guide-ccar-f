# National Energy Agency - Residential Heating Outlook 2025
Published: 2025-06-12 · Section references are given as §n

## §1 Adoption
As of Q4 2024, **13% of single-family homes** use a heat pump as their primary heating system,
up from 9% in 2022. Growth is concentrated in new construction and in states with rebate programs.

## §2 Installed cost
The median installed cost of a ducted air-source heat pump retrofit was **$14,000** in 2024
(n = 2,310 contractor invoices, rebates excluded).

## §3 Outlook
The agency projects 20-24% of single-family homes by 2030 under current policy.
