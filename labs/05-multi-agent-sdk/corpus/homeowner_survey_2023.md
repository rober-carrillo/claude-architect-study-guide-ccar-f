# Homeowner Comfort Survey 2023 (independent research group)
Fieldwork: March-April 2023 · Published: 2023-09-01

## Findings
- **9% of respondents** heat their home primarily with a heat pump (n = 4,800, weighted).
- 31% said they would "seriously consider" a heat pump at their next replacement.
- Cost was the top barrier, cited by 58%.

## Methodology
Online panel, single-family owner-occupiers only. Self-reported primary heating source.
